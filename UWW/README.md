# Ubiquitous Wearable Workshop 2025 presentation

The pupil-response individual-identification study was presented orally at the Ubiquitous Wearable Workshop 2025 at Kobe University on 19-20 December 2025. The workshop was organized by Kobe University's Tsukamoto-Terada Laboratory, associated with Professor Masahiko Tsukamoto, together with the NPO Wearable Computer Research and Development Organization.

Official workshop site: https://tt-lab.jp/uww2025/

The presentation covered the motivation for low-burden user identification, four visual-stimulus patterns and four color combinations from Experiment 1, the DBA/DTW comparison method, and the Experiment 1 EER evaluation. Later thesis materials extended the study to eight stimuli in Experiment 2.

The original deck, script, media, editable graphics, and source images are withheld. Their metadata and content included personal author information, an experiment photograph, individual pupil-response plots, or layered design data. This text records the presentation context without republishing those materials.
